Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 12, 2026 • NagarMitra

Welcome to NagarMitra ("we," "our," or "the Portal"). We value the privacy of our citizen users. This Privacy Policy details how personal information, complaint details, location data, and evidence photographs are collected, handled, and protected when you use our civic complaint management platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you use NagarMitra to submit, track, or manage public issues, we may collect the following data:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password hash, and optional phone number when you register as a citizen or administrator.
  • Civic Complaint Data: Category of issue (e.g., Potholes, Garbage, Street Light, Water/Sewage, Traffic Signal), issue description, date/time, and priority severity.
  • Location & Geodata: Street address, landmarks, and geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) provided via your browser's geolocation or manual address input.
  • Photographs / Media: Evidence photographs uploaded to demonstrate the civic issue.
  • Technical & Log Data: Browser type, operating system, IP address, referral UTM parameters, and device information for analytics and security.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for public grievance redressal purposes:

  • To route civic complaints to the responsible municipal department (e.g., PWD, Sanitation, Electricity Board, Jal Board).
  • To provide citizens with unique Complaint IDs to monitor real-time progress from submission to resolution.
  • To enable community upvoting and local civic accountability.
  • To notify citizens of resolution updates and administrative remarks.
  • To detect duplicate issues, spam, and maintain platform security.

3. Public vs. Private Information

To foster public accountability and community support, the following complaint details are made publicly visible on the portal: Complaint ID, problem category, description, approximate location, uploaded evidence photo, upvote count, and status timeline. Your private credentials, including your email address and password, are NEVER made publicly visible.

4. Local Storage & Cookies

NagarMitra uses browser LocalStorage to provide seamless client-side features, including complaint draft auto-save, authentication tokens, user preferences (dark mode and color themes), and cached complaint status. We do not use third-party tracking cookies to sell your data to advertisers.

5. Third-Party Integrations

  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim: Used for reverse geocoding to translate GPS coordinates into readable street addresses.
  • Google Analytics 4 & Google Ads: Used to understand aggregate portal usage metrics, measure feature adoption, and optimize awareness campaigns without personally identifying individual users.

6. Data Security

We employ standard security practices, including salted password hashing (bcrypt), JSON Web Tokens (JWT) for secure session authentication, and HTTPS transport encryption to safeguard your data against unauthorized access.

7. Citizen Rights

Citizens have the right to request deletion of their account, view their historical complaint logs, and update their personal details at any time through their dashboard or by contacting our support team.
